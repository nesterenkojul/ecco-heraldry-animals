# Symbolism of Animalistic Heraldry Supporters
## Based on the 18th-century catalogues from England, Ireland and Scotland
This project focuses on the heraldry depicted in the British printed publications of the 18th century. 
While many aspects of heraldry were strictly regulated, the animals used as supporters 
could be chosen and modified with considerable freedom. Given that these animals carry strong symbolism, 
we aimed to study which types of supporters were most common and 
how kingdom and societal status influenced these choices. 
Fortunately, we identified several valuable sources from the Eighteenth Century Collections Online (ECCO) 
database, including heraldry catalogues across Britain and a treatise detailing the distinct virtues 
associated with each animal supporter. Our solution combines data-driven image recognition 
and modern large language models for further understanding of heraldry.
